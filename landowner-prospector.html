What this tool does
County assessment records are public. This tool pulls every tax parcel around the towns you pick, groups parcels by owner, totals the acres, and keeps owners in your target range. It then screens each owner:
likely farmer Name looks like an operating farm ("… Farms Inc", grain/livestock words). These fail your "no farmers" rule — hide them with the filter.
managed / bank Mailing address is "c/o" someone, or a bank trust department is in the name — land likely under professional farm management (Hertz, First Mid Ag Services, Busey trust, etc.). Also usually a skip for you.
absentee Owner's mailing address is outside the local towns (or out of state). These are your best leads — non-farming heirs and investors who own the ground but don't work it.
trust / estate Family trusts and estates — often inherited ground, frequently open to a new operator or services.
entity LLC / partnership that isn't obviously a farm operation.
No public record says who farms a parcel or whether a farm manager is involved — the flags are honest heuristics, not facts. You know the names around Auburn: give the list a once-over and drop neighbors you know are operators before mailing.
County-by-county data routes
Sangamon — Auburn, Chatham, Pawnee, Divernon, Loami
Built in. The county's GIS server (gis.co.sangamon.il.us) serves the tax-parcel layer this tool queries. Cross-check any owner at the county's property search.
Macoupin — Virden, Girard
Viewer at macoupin-il.bhamaps.com and tax records at macoupinil.devnetwedge.com. The county also runs an open-data portal — if a parcel layer with an ArcGIS REST endpoint is published there, paste its URL into this tool ("Other county") and it works exactly like Sangamon.
Montgomery — Farmersville, Waggoner
On Beacon (Schneider) — no open API. Route: Beacon's advanced search → filter by acreage → work through Bois D'Arc & Pitman townships. Slower, but the same owner + mailing address data is there. A $50–100 county GIS export (GIS office, Old Courthouse, Hillsboro) gets you the whole parcel table at once.
Morgan — Waverly
County viewer runs on ArcGIS Online (county GIS page). Open the viewer, then in the browser address bar of any parcel popup's data source you'll find a …/FeatureServer/0 URL — paste it here as "Other county". Otherwise the assessor's office sells the parcel export.
From names to phone numbers
County data gives you the two things that matter most — legal owner name and mailing address. Landowner outreach runs on direct mail for a reason: it's the one channel that reaches every owner on the list. To add phones/emails:
1. Free people-search (TruePeopleSearch, FastPeopleSearch) — match name + city from your CSV; hit rate ~50–60% for individuals.
2. Paid skip-trace or list append (DataTree, USLandGrid, or any list broker) — $0.10–0.25/record, better for trusts.
3. AcreValue / Acres.com / LandGlide subscriptions bundle ownership + acreage + plat maps if you'd rather not touch raw county data at all.
Keep it CAN-SPAM/TCPA-clean: cold mail to landowners is unrestricted; cold calls to numbers on the Do-Not-Call registry are not, so scrub against DNC before dialing.
